Privacy Policy

We want making plans to feel easy, safe and transparent. That includes knowing exactly what happens with your data.

Last updated: 18 September 2026

👤 Who's responsible for bommi?

  • bommi is an independent project created and run by Melany María Trujillo Rivera, from Costa Rica. For now there's no company behind the app.
  • If you have a question about this policy, want to exercise any of your rights or wish to make a complaint, write to ayuda@bommiapp.com.
  • We review your request ourselves. There's no automated call centre and nobody in between.

📄 What information do we keep?

  • From your profile: name, age, description, photos, activities and interests, your profile answers, and the events or plans you like.
  • Private information, which never appears on your profile: your phone number, your date of birth and your approximate area.
  • Your activity inside bommi: who you sent a «¡Vamoos!» to, who you matched with, your messages, who you blocked and the reports you sent.
  • Your Instagram, if you choose to add it. It's entirely optional and you can remove it later.
  • We don't ask for your ID card, your passport or any identity document to create or use your account.

📍 How do we use your location?

  • We use it to tell you how many kilometres away each person is, and to avoid showing you people who are miles away.
  • The permission is given or removed from your phone's settings, not from bommi. If you say no, the app still works: you won't see distances and people will show up without that filter.
  • If you allow it, we don't store the exact point: before storing it we round it to a one-kilometre area. The exact point stays a few seconds on your phone while the distance is calculated and never reaches our servers.

🙂 How does verification work?

  • To verify that a person is who they say they are, we ask for a selfie taken inside the app.
  • The selfie is used only to compare it with your profile photos. It isn't published, it doesn't appear on your profile and no other user can see it.
  • Once the review is done, the selfie is deleted. We don't keep it as part of your account.
  • All we keep is the result — whether it was approved or not — and the date it was done.

👥 Who can see your information?

  • Your profile can be seen by other people using bommi: your name, your age, your photos, your description, your activities and interests, and whether you're verified.
  • We never show your phone number, your date of birth or your location.
  • Your messages are only seen by the people in that conversation. We don't read your chats routinely.
  • If someone reports a message or a conversation, we may review what's needed to investigate the report and take the appropriate action.

🚫 Things we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal data. Not to advertisers, not to companies, not to third parties.
  • bommi shows no advertising today. If we ever add it, we'll update this policy and tell you before the change applies.
  • We don't read your chats routinely. We only review a conversation when it's needed to handle a report, investigate a safety problem or comply with a legal obligation.
  • We don't follow you outside bommi. We don't look at what you do in other apps, websites or social networks.

🗄️ Where is your data stored?

  • We use Firebase, a Google service, to store bommi's information, including photos.
  • The servers may be outside Costa Rica, including the United States. That means your information may be transferred and stored outside your country.
  • Data is encrypted while it travels between your phone and our services, and stays encrypted while stored.

⏱️ How long do we keep your information?

  • While you have an active account, and for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations or handle safety matters. You can change or delete almost all of it from the app.
  • If you delete your account, we delete your photos, your profile, your phone number, your date of birth, your stored location and your «¡Vamoos!».
  • We can't delete messages that already reached someone else's phone. That conversation may stay with them, but your identity is unlinked: you appear as «User», without your name and without your photo.
  • Reports you sent may be kept even if you delete your account, when needed to investigate reported behaviour or prevent abuse. We keep the minimum and unlink it from your profile.
  • Deleting is immediate: there's no bin and no waiting. A trace may remain for a few more days in Google's automatic backups, until they recycle themselves.

✋ Your rights

  • You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, for corrections to anything wrong, for your data to be deleted where applicable, or for information about how we use it. Write to ayuda@bommiapp.com.
  • We'll answer within five working days at most, which is what the applicable law sets.
  • You can also delete your account yourself from Settings, whenever you want and without asking anyone.
  • These rights are exercised under Costa Rica's law No. 8968 and, where applicable, the data protection rules of the other countries where bommi operates.

🔞 bommi is for people 18 and over only

  • bommi is meant exclusively for people aged 18 or over. There are no exceptions.
  • If we find that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it and take whatever steps are needed to protect their safety and the community's.
  • If you're a parent or legal guardian and believe a minor is using bommi, write to ayuda@bommiapp.com.

We want bommi to be a place where you can discover plans, meet people and say «¡Vamoos!» without having to wonder what we're doing with your data. If you ever have a question, write to ayuda@bommiapp.com.

We may update this policy when the app changes, when the way we use data changes, or when legal obligations do. If the change is important, we'll tell you inside the app before it takes effect. We don't change it quietly.
