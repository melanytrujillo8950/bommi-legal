You can delete your account whenever you want, without asking anyone and without explaining why.

Last updated: 19 September 2026

📱 From the app, in a minute

  • Open bommi and go to Settings, the gear at the top of your profile.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap «Delete my account».
  • Confirm. It's deleted right away: no bin, no waiting and nothing to request.

✉️ If you no longer have the app

  • Write to ayuda@bommiapp.com from the email or with the phone number you opened the account with, so we can check it's you.
  • We answer within five working days at most, which is what Costa Rican law requires.
  • We won't ask you to reinstall the app or to explain why you're leaving.

🗑️ What gets deleted

  • Your photos, your profile, your description, your activities, your phone number, your date of birth, your stored location and your «¡Vamoos!».
  • We can't delete messages that already reached someone else's phone. That conversation stays with them, but without your name and without your photo: you appear as «User».
  • Reports you sent stay, without your profile, so what was reported can still be reviewed.
  • Deleting is immediate. A trace may remain for a few more days in Google's automatic backups, until they recycle themselves.

All of this is explained in more detail in our privacy policy.
