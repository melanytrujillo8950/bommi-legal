✨ What bommi is, and what it isn't

  • bommi is for finding someone to do something with: a concert, a mass, the beach, a coffee.
  • bommi puts you in touch with someone and that's where it ends. It doesn't organise the plan, it doesn't go with you and it isn't there when you meet.
  • We don't run background checks on anyone. Face verification says the photo is of that person; it doesn't say they're a good person. No app knows that.
  • By using bommi you accept these terms. If you disagree with any of them, don't use it — and if you already were, you can delete your account whenever you want.

👤 Who can use bommi

  • You have to be 18 or over. No exceptions, and lying about your age is grounds for closing your account without warning.
  • One account per person, with your real name and your real photos.
  • The account is yours and isn't lent or sold. What happens from your account is on you.
  • If someone impersonates another person, we close their account as soon as we find out.

⚠️ What isn't allowed

  • Harassing, threatening, insulting or pushing someone who already said no.
  • Sexual content: nudes, sexual messages to someone who didn't ask for them, or anything sexual involving minors. That last one we report to the authorities.
  • Drugs: selling them, offering them or looking for someone to get them with. Alcohol can be talked about — there are nightlife plans — but nothing illegal.
  • Selling, promoting your business, spamming, or using bommi to get clients or followers.
  • Asking another person on the app for money, bank details or passwords. If someone asks you, report it: it's the most common scam on apps like this.
  • Weapons, violence, hate for who someone is — their colour, their religion, their gender, who they love — and anything that's a crime.
  • Posting another person's photo, name, phone number or conversations without their permission.

🖼️ What you upload

  • Your photos and what you write stay yours. We don't keep anything.
  • All you give us is permission to show it inside bommi, which is what you uploaded it for. Outside the app we don't use it for anything.
  • Only upload photos of you, and photos you're allowed to upload. Someone else's photos, without their permission, no.

🚶 When you meet in person

  • The first time, in a public place with people around. Not at a house.
  • Tell someone you trust where you're going, who with and when you'll be back.
  • If something feels off, you leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
  • No app can guarantee the other person is trustworthy, and bommi can't either. What we do is require face verification, let you block and report, and review what gets reported.

🚩 If someone behaves badly

  • Report them. From their profile, or by holding down the message if it was something they wrote to you.
  • The reported person is never told who reported them. Never.
  • We look at every report, and the serious ones first: threats, weapons, scams, sexual content without consent, or an account that looks like it belongs to a minor.
  • Depending on what we find, it may end in nothing, in a warning, or in that person leaving bommi. What decides it isn't how many reports they have, but what happened.

❌ When we can close an account

  • When someone breaks the rules above, we close it. Depending on how serious, with or without warning.
  • If it's something that puts someone in danger, or it looks like a minor's account, we close it right away and with no refunds.
  • If you think we closed your account by mistake, write to ayuda@bommiapp.com and we'll look at it again. We can get things wrong.

💎 Premium and payments

  • bommi is free to use. Premium is optional and only changes how much you see and how much you're seen: it never buys anyone's attention.
  • Premium is a subscription paid through the store — Apple or Google — and it renews itself until you cancel. You see the price there before paying.
  • You cancel from the store, not from bommi, and you keep Premium until the time you already paid for runs out.
  • Refunds are handled by the store, not by us: on iPhone you ask Apple, and on Android, Google refunds if you ask within the first 48 hours.
  • For now Premium isn't charged yet. When it starts, we'll tell you inside the app first.
  • Paying for Premium doesn't entitle you to an answer from anyone. Nobody owes anybody anything here.

✋ Leaving whenever you want

  • You can delete your account from Settings, without asking anyone and without explaining anything.
  • Deleting it removes your profile, your photos and your data. What stays, and why, is in the privacy policy.
  • We could also close bommi one day. If that happens, we'll tell you in time so you can take your things out.

If we change anything important here, we tell you inside the app before it takes effect. We don't change it quietly.
