✨ What is bommi, and what isn't it?

  • bommi is an app for finding people to share plans and activities with in person: a concert, a mass, a beach day, a coffee, a film, a hike, a match or a night out.
  • bommi makes the meeting possible, and that's where our part ends: it doesn't organise the plan for you, it doesn't go with anyone when they meet, and it takes no part in the meeting.
  • We can't guarantee how another person will behave or that they're trustworthy. Verification checks that the photos match that person; it doesn't mean bommi considers them good or safe. No app can know someone's intentions.
  • By creating an account or using bommi, you accept these Terms. If you don't agree with them, you shouldn't use the app; and if you already have an account, you can delete it at any time from Settings.

👤 Who can use bommi?

  • bommi is exclusively for people aged 18 or over. There are no exceptions. Deliberately lying about your age can result in your account being closed without prior notice.
  • When you sign up you must give truthful information and use photos that are of you.
  • One person, one account. You can't create accounts for other people, or share, lend, sell or transfer yours, or pretend to be someone else.
  • Activity from your account is treated as done by you, so keep your login details safe.

⚠️ What isn't allowed

  • Harassing, stalking, threatening, insulting or humiliating. Nor continuing to contact someone who has made clear they don't want it. A «no» is enough.
  • Posting nudes or explicit sexual content, sending unsolicited sexual content, or pressuring someone to send intimate content.
  • Any sexual content involving minors. These situations may be reported to the relevant authorities.
  • Selling, offering, buying or looking for drugs. Nor using bommi to put people in touch for that purpose. Talking about alcohol can be part of nightlife plans, as long as the activity is legal.
  • Asking for money, bank details, passwords or security codes; deceiving someone to obtain money or information; promoting a business, getting clients or sending spam. If someone asks you for money or passwords, report it.
  • Promoting or threatening violence, planning or promoting crimes, and buying, selling or trading weapons through bommi.
  • Attacking, threatening or promoting violence against a person or group because of their skin colour, origin, religion, gender, sexual orientation or other personal characteristics.
  • Posting someone else's photos, name, phone number, conversations or screenshots without permission. Someone sending you something inside bommi doesn't mean you're allowed to publish it outside.

🖼️ What you post on bommi

  • Your photos, text and other content stay yours. We don't become the owners of what you post.
  • When you upload something you only give us the permission needed to show, store and use it inside the app, so bommi's features can work.
  • Only post content that is yours or that you have permission to use, that doesn't infringe anyone else's rights and that isn't illegal.

🚶 When you meet in person

  • For the first meeting, pick a public place with other people around. Avoid a house, a hotel or an isolated place the first time.
  • Tell someone you trust where you're going, who with and when you expect to be back.
  • If anything makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe during the meeting, you can leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.
  • Verification can help confirm an identity, but it doesn't guarantee that a person is safe or trustworthy. That's why you can also block, report accounts and report messages.

🚩 What do I do if someone behaves badly?

  • Report them. You can do it from the person's profile or by holding down a message.
  • We don't tell the reported person who made the report, unless a legal obligation requires us to.
  • We review reports and prioritise anything that may be a serious risk: threats, violence, weapons, scams, unsolicited sexual content, or the possible involvement of a minor.
  • After reviewing a case we may take no action, send a warning, limit features, suspend or close the account. Action doesn't depend on how many reports someone has: what matters is what happened and how serious it was.

❌ When can we close an account?

  • When someone breaks these Terms or the community guidelines, gives false information on purpose, impersonates someone, uses bommi for illegal activity, poses a serious risk to others, is under 18, or tries to get around a previous closure by creating another account.
  • Depending on how serious it is, we may do it with or without prior notice. Where there's an immediate risk to others, or we believe an account belongs to a minor, we'll close it right away.
  • We can also get it wrong. If you think your account was closed by mistake, write to ayuda@bommiapp.com and we'll review your case.

💎 Premium and payments

  • bommi can be used for free. Premium is optional and only changes how much you can see and how much your profile can appear: it never buys anyone's attention, a reply, a match or their company.
  • When it's available, Premium will be a subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. The price and conditions are shown before you buy.
  • You cancel from your Apple App Store or Google Play account, and you keep access until the period you already paid for ends.
  • Purchases are handled under those platforms' terms, and cancellations and refunds are subject to their rules and procedures, not ours.
  • For now Premium has no active charge. If we start charging, we'll tell you inside the app first.

✋ You can leave whenever you want

  • You can delete your account from Settings at any time. You don't need our permission and you don't have to explain why.
  • When you delete it, our Privacy Policy applies regarding the deletion and retention of your data.
  • We may also stop offering bommi in the future. If that happens, we'll try to tell you in advance and we'll meet any obligations regarding active services or payments.
